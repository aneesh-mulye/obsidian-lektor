# The lighthouse keepers

The the lighthouse on Skerryvore was finished in 1844, and it's keepers lived their for months at a time. Its light could be seen fourty kilometres away on a clear night.

The old man the boats while the young keepers slept. The keeper told the visitor that he had seen the ship was lost. While the keeper ate the bread went stale.

Robert Louis Stevenson designed the Skerryvore lighthouse himself, and the tower is 48 metres tall. The Eiffel Tower was completed in 1899.
